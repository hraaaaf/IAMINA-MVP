## Lexical ambiguity review

No **under-triage** gap was identified in the exact committed trigger universe. Several standalone tokens are intentionally broad and can conservatively over-triage outside a richer glucose context. Examples include French `malaise`, `vertige(s)`, `confusion`; Arabic `دوخة`; and Darija/transliterated dizziness or distress forms such as `dwekh`, `dwakht`, `dayakht`, `dawkhani`, `ma kan7ml` / `ma kan7mlch`.

**Classification:** non-blocking for this AI secondary safety review because the failure mode is conservative escalation rather than missed high-severity routing. It remains a product-specificity / false-positive concern and must not be reinterpreted as native-language naturalness approval.

Some transliterations are inherently non-standard. Their exact linguistic naturalness and regional acceptability therefore remain part of the native-human gate even though their current presence does not weaken deterministic safety containment.
