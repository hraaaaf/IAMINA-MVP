## Goal / observable success
Patient controls voluntary criteria and can clearly refuse or revoke; no field alone automatically enables consent. OFF when no row in physically selected patient vault, four numbers empty by default, precise kg fixed-point and positive days, active source patient only, A→B→A switch does not leak data. Explicit checked agreement precedes durable `optIn`. `optOut` deletes criteria. **No notification is sent or scheduled**, no OS permission request, no model call, no LLM/medical threshold/diagnosis. UI explicitly says actual sending is unavailable in this build.
