Times of Oman independently describes Al Madhbi as a food for which Dhofar is famous, prepared by grilling meat on traditionally heated stones: https://timesofoman.com/article/148133-tourists-get-a-unique-dining-experience-in-dhofar-during-the-khareef-season .
- Maajeen: Oman Observer lists Ma'ajeen among traditional Dhofar foods prepared for locals and visitors: https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1156836/oman/unique-culinary-delights-of-dhofar-during-khareef . Times of Oman independently identifies Muajeen/Ma'ajeen as a famous local Dhofar meal and describes its traditional meat/fat preparation: https://timesofoman.com/article/148133-tourists-get-a-unique-dining-experience-in-dhofar-during-the-khareef-season .
