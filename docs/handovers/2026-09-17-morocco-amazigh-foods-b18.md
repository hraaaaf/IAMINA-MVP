# FoodPicker B18 — Morocco Amazigh depth

Goal: append three independently selectable Moroccan concepts to certified B17 (353) without altering prior entries/order.

Accepted: `tagoula`, `ourkimen`, `tehal_stuffed_spleen` → target 356.

Evidence:
- Tagoula: Yabiladi, https://www.yabiladi.com/articles/details/158273/yennayer-tagoula-cialit-ancestrale-nouvel.html — ancestral Amazigh dish, especially Souss/Tiznit, corn/barley semolina; article explicitly attributes Tiznit culinary-tradition positioning to ONMT. Morocco World News, https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/01/274981/celebrating-yennayer-the-flavors-of-the-amazigh-new-year-in-morocco/ — traditional Amazigh dish from southern Morocco/Souss, barley or corn porridge associated with Yennayer.
- Ourkimen: Morocco World News, same 2026 Yennayer feature — Souss Yennayer dish of soaked legumes/grains including lentils, barley, wheat, beans and chickpeas. Hespress English, https://en.hespress.com/100752-yennayer-shows-the-power-of-amazigh-identity-now-advocates-are-making-moroccos-political-inaction-impossible-to-ignore.html — Yennayer dish described as slow-cooked dried legumes and grains. The two sources agree on identity and core composition.
- Tehal/Tihane: Journal of Ethnic Foods / ScienceDirect DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jef.2018.02.004 — peer-reviewed overview identifies Tehal/tehane as a Moroccan traditional meat specialty: stuffed bovine/ovine spleen with ground meat, olives, fat and spices, baked then sliced/griddled. TasteAtlas, https://www.tasteatlas.com/stuffed-camel-spleen — Moroccan traditional stuffed-spleen specialty; supporting culinary reference: https://tasteofmaroc.com/moroccan-stuffed-spleen-tihane-farcie/.

Rejected during research:
- `zamita` — official Terroir du Maroc describes it as a variant of Sellou; existing catalog already contains `sellou`, so it fails the independent-concept / near-synonym gate.
- `baddaz` — culturally plausible maize couscous, but this cycle did not obtain two sufficiently strong provenance sources relative to the accepted set; deferred rather than lowering evidence quality.
